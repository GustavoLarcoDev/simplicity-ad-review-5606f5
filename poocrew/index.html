What I’ve learned

The whole team reads this before making any new piece

Avoid

  • Prices in new pieces until the new price list is confirmed. Gustavo: "the price changed recently" (H11, H21, H25, H28, 2026-09-17). Do not use $65, $80, $90, $100, "+$10 per dog" or "$100 flat" in images or copy until CLAUDE.md has the new prices. The website still shows the old ones. High confidence (said by Gustavo).
  • Empty space around the subject. Crop tight so the dogs, or the tent, fill the frame. No big stretches of sky, lawn, asphalt or background above, below or beside the subject just to hold text. Gustavo on G10, G12 and H10 ("too much empty space, crop"), and H27 ("cut the right side to focus on the tent"). High confidence (said by Gustavo, 4 pieces).
  • Explaining which dog is which ("Max is the chocolate one. Simon is the black one."). Not needed. Gustavo on G10. High confidence (said by Gustavo).
  • Solid color blocks in the top half of the piece, meaning brown or red headers or panels up top. High confidence: A9 (red block on top), G7 and G9 (solid brown header), all rejected.
  • Flat backgrounds with the photo shrunk into a card, or the photo used as decoration for a graphic layout. High confidence: G8 (cream background, photo in a card), plus A2, A5 and A7 (typography or grids with no hero photo).
  • Empty space reserved for stickers or polls. A story must never look unfinished. If a sticker is needed, place it on a photo piece that already works. Medium confidence: G8, G9.

Repeat

  • The real photo, full bleed, as the hero, with text on a soft gradient, away from faces. High confidence: G2, G4, G5, G6, G11, H3, H7, H13, H14, H19 accepted, plus A1.
  • Tight detail and close-up crops of the dogs: paws (H22), collar buckle (H23), Simon sniffing (H26), faces (H5, H8, H12). High confidence: all accepted.
  • Real team members at the event booth. High confidence: G6 and H24 accepted in two batches.
  • If there is a panel, it goes at the bottom and blends into the photo, or use the series C frame (photo, red rule, brown panel at the bottom). High confidence: G1, G3, H5, H12, H17 accepted.
  • Big Archivo Black headline, few words, contact info in gold. High confidence: G1–G6 and H batch accepted.
  • Dry humor over a real, everyday photo of the dogs ("Max did his part.", "Nothing left to find."). High confidence: G5, H26 accepted.
  • The same concept in 4:5 and full-bleed 9:16, instead of designing a separate story. Medium confidence: the 9:16 versions of G2, G3 and G6 accepted, plus G11.
  • Price guarantee only with its full conditions visible (H17). Medium confidence.
  • A soft, portrait-mode background blur is OK to tone down background clutter such as parked trucks (H7). Medium confidence.
  • Print with the real photo full bleed on top, a big offer and a QR code (flyer R, rack card R). Medium confidence.
Pillow composites on real photos. Nothing gets posted or sent without your OK.
