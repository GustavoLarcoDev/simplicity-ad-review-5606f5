Signs your business has outgrown spreadsheets

Mon 21 · 8:00
Post text 
Spreadsheets are where a lot of good businesses start.
They aren't always built for where the business goes next.

A few signs it may be time for something built around how you work:

– The same information lives in more than one place, and nobody is sure which version is current.
– Only one person really understands how the file works.
– Your team copies data by hand between tools that don't talk to each other.
– People work around off-the-shelf software, instead of it working for them.
– Customers or staff need something on their phones, and a spreadsheet isn't made for that.
– Simple questions about your own business are hard to answer.
– You've heard a lot about AI, but you're not sure where it fits in your business.

None of this means the spreadsheet failed. It usually means the business grew.

Through our Custom Software service, we build web, mobile and AI tools around the way you already work. The goal is Simplicity First: fewer workarounds, not more technology.

If a few of these sound familiar, it may be worth a conversation.
