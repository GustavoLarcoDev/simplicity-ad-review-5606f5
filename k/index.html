Five styles with four ads each, on purpose: what you approve or reject teaches which style, tone and service you like best. All in 4:5 for Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn, with the bigger logo you asked for on G1.

How Claude listens. While the Claude Code terminal stays open, Claude checks this gallery every 5 minutes. Approve copies the piece into a dated Posts folder (e.g. Posts-2026-09-17). Reject deletes it and removes it from this page. Adjust has Claude remake it following your comment and bring it back here as a new, unmarked version. Every decision is logged in aprendizaje/preferencias.md, which the team reads before designing: what you reject is not proposed the same way again, and what you ask to adjust is applied to new pieces. Write your comment before marking, or within 2 minutes after; when you reject, saying why helps it learn better.
